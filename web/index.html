StitchWright

Turn a simple line drawing into a file your embroidery machine can stitch.

Everything runs on your device. Your image is never uploaded.

Starting up…

The first visit downloads the converter (about 30 MB). After that it’s saved on your device, loads instantly, and works offline.

            Running traces each line with a single row of
            stitches — best for outlines, sketches and fine detail.
            Satin fills each line with dense side-to-side
            stitches for a solid, slightly raised look — best for lettering
            and bold shapes. Fill covers a solid/filled
            shape with dense parallel rows — best for filled blobs,
            block letters and other large solid areas (not thin outlines).
          

        


        

          
          
The height adjusts on its own to keep the shape.

          

            
            
          

        


        

          
          

            The hoop is the frame that clamps your fabric in the machine. Each
            one has a maximum stitch area — set by your machine and the
            hoop you own, and listed in the machine’s manual (often printed
            on the hoop itself, as width × height like 4×4″).
            We’ll warn you if the design won’t fit the one you pick.

            sticker sheets and alternating patterns.
